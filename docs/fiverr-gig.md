## Title (max 80 characters)
I will build product recommendations and bundles from your store's order data

## Description
Your order history already shows which products sell together and what each customer is likely to buy next. I'll turn your Shopify, WooCommerce or POS export into recommendations you can use straight away:

✅ **Frequently bought together** pairs for your product pages
✅ **Bundle ideas** your customers already buy together
✅ **Personal picks for each customer**, each with a reason ("because you bought…")
✅ **Campaign lists:** who to target for any product
✅ **Win-back list:** your best lapsed customers, with the products most likely to bring them back

**Tested on your own data first.** I hide your most recent orders, predict them, and show you how often the recommendations were right, compared with simply showing best-sellers. On a real UK gift shop with about 1 million order lines, the engine found a correct new product for 46% of returning customers, 1.5× better than best-sellers.

**Your data stays private.** It's used only for your project and deleted after delivery.

**What I need from you:** an orders export (CSV or Excel) with order number, product name or SKU, quantity and date, plus customer email or phone if you want personal picks.

## FAQ
**Which platforms do you support?** Shopify and WooCommerce exports work directly; any POS or spreadsheet works if it has one row per product per order.

**Do I need to install anything?** No. You send the export and receive Excel files.

**How much history is needed?** At least 3–6 months is ideal. Personal picks need at least 50 repeat customers; with fewer, I'll focus on bought-together pairs and bundles and tell you honestly.

**Can you put the recommendations on my website?** The Premium report includes ready-to-use lists. Integration into your theme can be quoted separately.
